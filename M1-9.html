Common Types of Fake Paragraphs

• Lorem Ipsum: Classic scrambled Latin text derived from Cicero's De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum, used since the 1960s to mimic natural word and letter distribution without distracting readers.

• Microsoft Word Shortcut Text: You can automatically generate fake text in Microsoft Word by typing =rand(p,s) (where p is the number of paragraphs and s is sentences per paragraph) or =lorem(p,s) for Latin text.

• Custom Dummy Generators: Tools like the Blind Text Generator let you create custom blocks of text, alternating between classic placeholder copy and standard English.
